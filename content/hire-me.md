# Featured Works

## Kiva and the Maglev Heist
A one-shot, systems-lite TTRPG made for my cyberpunk setting. Others in this setting include Varth's Veingud Vaxis and Fungal Scavengers. It contains a rules system, races, classes, ready-made player characters, NPCs and enemy unitys, encounters and more.

## Scraps and Slaps
A post-apocalyptic location supplement for GMs. It includes NPCs, rules for a minigame, environmental descriptions, and GM advice.

## The City of Caidour
A fantasy one-shot designed for the Dungeons and Dragons system. It includes over 15 locations, a major quest, three minor quests, 15 NPCs, a dungeon with environmental puzzles, and statblocks for two custom enemies.
