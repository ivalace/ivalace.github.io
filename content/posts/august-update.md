# August Update: Texas is still hotter
_Tokyo is still pretty hot though_
You know, everyone kept warning me about Tokyo summers. The horror, the heat, the humidity. You’ll never survive, you’ll never recover, it’s the worst in the world… which it’s not pleasant. I certainly could do without the oppressive weight of air that contains more water than oxygen.

However, it’s nothing compared to growing up with +40C and 70% humidity in east Texas. So I’ll take that as a win.

That being said, I still feel like it’s been a slow month, despite how busy I’ve been. Probably the most fun thing I’ve done this month is joined a Taiko school! Look at my beautiful drums. Aren’t they pretty~

![](/photos/taiko.jpeg "My taiko drums")

I go once a week and take out all my frustrations on these bad boys. I don’t know anything more satisfying…

Except maybe publishing my new book!
