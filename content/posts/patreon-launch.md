# The Tiers

## The Tip Vial - $3

The tip jar tier. Please enable my coffee addiction. You get my undying gratitude!

## Archivist - $5

As an archivist, you get all content available one week early. Public posts go up on the last day of the month. This includes the monthly supplement, as well as any additional works I make that month.

You also get voting power! Sometimes (all the time) I have multiple ideas and can’t decide what to work on next. Take away the cognitive load and make the choice for me!

There will also be Patreon specific updates, occasional art sprites and animations for video games I’m working on, and other fun sporadic things for members only.

## The Lore Hoard - $10

Yessss at last we get to the big one~ The Lore Hoard includes all the other stuff, your name in the credits section of all publications, a hand written thank you note mailed to you on Japanese stationary and Hoard Drops!

## Hoard Drops

Hoard drops are fun thing I’ve been planning for some time, and was only waiting on visa stuff to allow me to publish things for money. These will be seasonally curated collections of everything I’ve made (paid and free) that quarter in one pretty package. All supplements, stat blocks, random NPCs and items, everything all conveniently organized in a single PDF. They will cost anywhere from $15-20, with an optional add on of maps, sprites, and other visual art pieces for an extra $5. For Lore Hoard members, this is FREE and available a week early.

The first Hoard Drop will happen in December, I’m aiming for around the 20th to the 28th for all Lore Hoard members. It will be available for purchase by the public on December 31st for $15, with an additional art add on for another $5.

Over the next month I have a variety of posts scheduled, with the first early release set to launch September 23rd! The Red Marshes will be available to the public on September 30th. Additionally, a small collection of NPCs will be going public on the 30th for $2. All Lore Hoard members will get the PDF for free same day.

The first 10 members at any paid level will also get a unique 3D card made in Japan with a hand written thank you note! For those of you who don’t know, Japan makes some truly spectacular cards. And stationary in general for that matter.
