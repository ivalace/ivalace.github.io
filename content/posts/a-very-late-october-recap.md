Hello there!

I am… so very tired. But good! But tired. October was a blur of releasing our first game, creating the Hazy Glow Games Collective, making a Nagito Komaeda jacket pattern, making the jacket itself, running events, and sooooo much more that I didn’t plan for.

But! We persist so let’s get into it. The biggest news from this month first, because that’s what I’m most excited about.

## Hazy Glow Games Collective

Yes, the fruition of over 15 years of dreaming and wanting and planning, my wife and I finally launched our own indie collective. The goal is simple: have fun making games with others who want to have fun making games. Our first game, Thorge, involved the two of us with additional art from my brother in law. Our next game, Cursed Cafe, will involve the two of us and my friend [Lauren M Crown](https://www.patreon.com/c/crown_of_ink/posts), who came up with the idea and is doing all the art for it. Another one is a werewolf-inspired digital card game called The King is Dead with our friend Andreas.

I’m sure I’ll do a longer, more involved post explaining Hazy Glow Games eventually, but as this is the Inkwell Archives, I’ll save that for another space. You can follow our budding collective on [Itch.io](https://hazy-glow-games.itch.io), [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/hazyglowgames.bsky.social), [Newgrounds](https://hazyglowgames.newgrounds.com), and [Mastodon](https://mastodon.gamedev.place/@hazyglowgames).

## Thorge

Thorge, a game mentioned above, released mid-October and was the first game I’ve ever done art for! Originally part of a LowRes game jam, the entire game had to be played within a 64x64 pixel square. Which meant all assets had to be made within that constraint. Given such a small resolution, and the fact that this is my first time doing pixel art, I’m pretty proud of what I managed to make!

## What else did I do this October…

Oh yeah. A lot.

I ran a workshop for Tokyo Game Bridge called Prompt to Play, where we had people bring in their ideas and create two usable assets for a game from a single prompt. Some walked away with character sprites, some walked away with settings, some walked away with tokens. We had people making cards, pixel art, hand drawn assets, all kinds of cool art that can be slotted directly into their games in whatever medium they chose. All in all, everyone had a good time and learned something, so I’d count that as a success!

#### Commissions

Yes! You there, with the face! You lucky being of the physical realm. I am opening my doors to commissions for TTRPG work and (if you live in the Japan) custom clothing. I received my first ever commission recently for a dress, and then a cosplay jacket for Nagito Komaeda from Dangan Ronpa. It’s still a work in progress, but currently I’d say I’m about 85% done with it.

![](/photos/october-recap-2026/nagito-jacket-progress-01.jpeg)

I first took her measurements and began drafting the design in my sketchbook. Then I started with a basic bodice and sleeve block made with her measurements, adding the jacket-specific details as I went.

![](/photos/october-recap-2026/nagito-jacket-progress-02.jpeg)

Then I made a couple of mock ups with similar fabric, doing fit checks and making minor adjustments to things like sleeve length and the pyramid shaping on the back. Then it’s as simple as cutting and piecing the fabric together! Thankfully 100% cotton is much easier to work with than glittery stretch velvet.

My birthday dress still gives me nightmares.

![](/photos/october-recap-2026/nagito-jacket-progress-03.jpeg)

I ran out of paint part of the way through the detailing on the back, so the 55 is, alas, not as potent as I’d like yet. A couple more layers and it’ll be good.
